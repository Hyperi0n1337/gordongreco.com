# Content inventory: old → new

| Journey | Supplied copy | Implemented copy | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home H1 | “Better information for the decisions that shape wealth.” | “Financial decisions are connected. Your analysis should be too.” | Names the problem and differentiator without vague “better” language. |
| Home descriptor | “Fee-Only Fiduciary Advisory” | “Pre-registration planning & research” | Aligns the hero with the supplied disclosure that the firm is not registered. |
| Home audience | HNW clients implied across cards/footer | Business owners, complex families, pre-retirees/retirees, and US/Greece households named near the top | Improves self-identification and mobile comprehension. |
| Home proof | IBKR scale, custody, SIPC, and fiduciary-by-law claims | Transparent work-product structure: decision, options, assumptions, action sequence | Removes unverified regulated and third-party claims; replaces adjective-based trust with inspectable outputs. |
| Home process | Multiple competing CTAs and a pilot paragraph | Fit call → written scope → analysis review | Answers “what happens next” in three steps. |
| Fees | “1% AUM annually” presented as an active advisory fee | Planned 1% annual management fee, contingent on verified registration and agreement; scoped research quoted in writing | Preserves supplied fee intent without pretending an active regulated service exists. |
| Services H1 | “Comprehensive Wealth Management” | “Choose the decision, not a package.” | Reduces generic category language and reframes navigation around user intent. |
| Services comparison | Five repeated cards with “Learn More” | One-click situation views plus output/fee/boundary cards | Clarifies which workstream relates to a business owner, retiree, or cross-border household. |
| About H1 | “A Fiduciary With an Engineering Mindset” | “Built for decisions that cross tax, investments, and business.” | Keeps the engineering method while removing an unverified legal status claim. |
| Founder proof | Broad international/fiduciary claims | Only supplied facts: Markos Terzidis, engineering mindset, US/Greece perspective, decision modeling | Prevents invented credentials or outcomes. |
| Contact H1 | “Let’s Talk” | “Start with one decision.” | Gives prospects a concrete prompt and reduces blank-page anxiety. |
| Contact schedule | Embedded scheduler with competing form | External scheduler as primary action; concise form as secondary route | Reduces third-party payload and CTA competition while preserving existing routes. |
| Contact safety | No prominent sensitive-data warning | Repeated “no account numbers, credentials, or documents” guidance | Protects prospects and preserves portal boundary. |
| Client H1 | “A private client space, built with care.” | “Invited client? Use the secure link in your invitation.” | Routes invited clients directly without implying public authentication exists. |
| Client controls | No fake login, but future-state copy led the page | Explicit “portal not enabled” status, invitation steps, and advisor contact | Makes the current action and unavailable state unmistakable. |
| Service detail pages | Capability-heavy claims and repeated footer language | Best-fit signals, questions, useful output, exclusions, fee, and connected work | Addresses objections and turns each page into a concise decision journey. |
| Legal/footer | Contradictory “fiduciary by law” and “not registered” claims | One consistent pre-registration disclosure | Eliminates the highest-risk credibility contradiction. |

## Service-detail and policy pages

| Journey | Baseline copy | Implemented copy | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business | “Focus on Running Your Business.” | “Run the business and household from the same facts.” | Makes the differentiating coordination problem explicit and avoids promising execution services. |
| Tax | “Year-Round, Not Filing Season.” | “Model tax choices before the deadline makes them for you.” | Reframes the page around decisions and timing while removing implied tax-preparation authority. |
| Investment | “Built Around Your Tax Situation.” | “Make portfolio choices in the context of taxes and cash needs.” | Identifies the useful connection and stops implying active management before registration is verified. |
| Retirement | “Stress-Tested Against Thousands of Scenarios.” | “Sequence income, taxes, and risk across retirement.” | Removes an unverified quantity claim and states the planning decision in plain language. |
| Estate | “Protecting What You’ve Built.” | “Give your attorney a clearer financial fact pattern.” | Defines the advisor/counsel handoff and avoids sounding like legal drafting. |
| Privacy | Policy referenced Form ADV, formal advisory engagement, Regulation S-P, and settled provider settings. | Public-site-only policy with sensitive-data prohibition and explicit operational-verification notes. | Aligns the policy with pre-registration status and avoids claiming unverified backend practices. |
| Terms | Fiduciary-by-law and Form ADV statements conflicted with the footer’s unregistered disclosure. | One pre-registration status, contingent fee terms, no-results guarantee, and professional coordination limits. | Removes the core legal contradiction without inventing registration details. |
