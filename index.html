Tax-Alpha Strategies

Every basis point matters. Here's how we generate measurable, after-tax value for your portfolio.

Tax-Loss Harvesting

Systematically harvest portfolio losses to offset gains, reducing your annual tax bill while maintaining market exposure.

Asset Location

Place tax-inefficient assets (bonds, REITs) in tax-deferred accounts and tax-efficient assets (index funds) in taxable — saving 15–25 basis points annually.

Roth Conversion

Strategic bracket-filling conversions from traditional IRA to Roth, locking in today's tax rates against future increases.

QDI Optimization

Maximize qualified dividend income to benefit from the lower long-term capital gains rate instead of ordinary income rates.

Estate & Trust Planning

Trust structure optimization, accumulation vs. distribution analysis, and multi-generational tax planning for complex family situations.

Portfolio Construction

Evidence-based allocation using low-cost index funds, factor tilts, and Section 1256 contracts where appropriate for 60/40 tax treatment.
