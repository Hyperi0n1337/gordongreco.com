Retirement planning

Sequence income, taxes, and risk across retirement.

Compare contribution, claiming, spending, and withdrawal choices across time while making uncertainty and assumptions visible.

Discuss this decisionCompare all services →

Best fit when

  • Retirement is within sight or already underway
  • Social Security and withdrawal timing interact
  • Roth conversion or contribution choices need context
  • Spending, inheritance, or care assumptions are changing

Questions the work can clarify

  1. Which income sources fund each stage, and with what tax effect?
  2. How do claiming and conversion options compare under stated assumptions?
  3. Which scenarios create the most pressure on liquidity or portfolio risk?
  4. What decisions have deadlines, and which can wait?
