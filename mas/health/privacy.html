MAS health connector privacy policy

Updated 6 November 2026. This policy describes the MAS Google Health connector, not other Google integrations or the Gordon Greco website.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's consent, the connector requests read-only access to Google Health activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep. It also reads the Google Health identity needed to bind observations to the intended account. Data is used for personal health-history review, calculations and planning in MAS.

Storage and disclosure

Authorized records and authorization credentials are held in separately configured, owner-managed MAS storage. Records may be included in the owner's managed backups. This connector does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or publish health records on this website. Any separate disclosure to another person or service requires the owner's applicable authorization; connecting Google Health is not blanket authorization to share it.

MAS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control, retention and removal

The owner may revoke the application's Google access in Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not by itself erase previously stored records or backup copies. Existing records remain under the owner's data-management controls until removed. This policy does not claim an automatic retention deadline.
