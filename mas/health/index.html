MAS health connector

Markos Analytics Suite (MAS) is a personally operated system for reviewing health and activity observations and supporting personal planning.

Its Google Health connector reads the owner's authorized activity and fitness data, health metrics and measurements, and sleep records. These observations feed personal history views and planning tools. It does not provide a public health-data portal or invite unrestricted users.

This connector uses an installed desktop OAuth client. Authorization does not grant access to Gmail, and the connector does not request Gmail permissions.

Read the privacy policy and terms before authorizing access.
