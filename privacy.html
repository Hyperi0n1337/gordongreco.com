Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 22, 2026

Gordon Greco LLC ("we", "our", "the firm") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. This policy applies only to gordongreco.com and the inquiry channels it exposes. Information collected in the course of a formal advisory engagement is governed separately by our Form ADV Part 2A, our advisory agreement, and applicable financial privacy regulations (including Regulation S-P).

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what you choose to submit through the website, plus basic technical data that your browser sends automatically:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, and the contents of any message you send us through the contact form on gordongreco.com/contact.html.
  • Scheduling data — if you book a call using the embedded scheduler, Cal.com collects the name, email, and meeting time you provide.
  • Usage analytics — Google Analytics 4 logs aggregated, anonymized information about how visitors use the site (pages viewed, referring source, device type, approximate geography). We do not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or cross-site tracking.

We do not collect Social Security numbers, account numbers, financial statements, or tax documents through this website. Those are exchanged only after you engage us under a signed advisory agreement, through encrypted channels we confirm with you.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and decide, with you, whether our services are the right fit.
  • To schedule and prepare for an introductory call.
  • To understand site performance in aggregate — which pages are helpful, which are not — and improve them.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

3. Third-Party Services

A few vendors operate parts of this website on our behalf. Each has its own privacy policy, which governs how they handle the data you submit:

  • Formspree — relays contact-form submissions to our email inbox. Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Cal.com — handles scheduling and calendar availability. Cal.com Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics 4 — provides aggregated traffic analytics. IP addresses are anonymized. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting — the site is served as a static site via Netlify / GitHub Pages. Server logs (IP, user-agent, timestamp) are retained briefly for abuse prevention.

4. Data Retention

Contact inquiries are retained in our email for up to 90 days and then deleted unless an advisory relationship has been established or you ask us to keep them. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics defaults (currently 14 months) at an aggregated level only. Scheduling data is retained per Cal.com's defaults.

5. Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Confirm what inquiry data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your inquiry and any record of it.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

To exercise any of these rights, email markos@gordongreco.com. We respond within five business days.

6. Security

The website is served exclusively over HTTPS. Contact-form content travels encrypted to Formspree and to our email. We never ask for sensitive financial information through the website.

7. Children

This website is directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page reflects the most recent update. Material changes will be flagged on this page for at least 30 days.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email markos@gordongreco.com.
