Estate & Trust Planning

Protecting What
You've Built.

Protecting what you've built — with structures that work for your family, not against them.

Trust Strategy

The right trust structure can protect your assets, reduce taxes, and preserve benefits for vulnerable family members. The wrong one can do the opposite. We help you understand the tradeoffs clearly — before any decisions are made.

  • Analysis of accumulate-in-trust vs. distribute-to-beneficiary — optimizing for total tax across all entities
  • Revocable and irrevocable trust design considerations
  • Special Needs Trusts (SNT) with benefit safety analysis — ensuring distributions don't jeopardize SSI or Medicaid
  • Qualified Disability Trusts (QDT) and their tax advantages

Family Tax Optimization

Families have more tax planning tools available than most individuals realize. Income that would be taxed heavily in one member's hands may be taxed lightly — or not at all — in another's.

  • Income shifting strategies to lower-bracket family members
  • Kiddie tax awareness and planning
  • Joint filing vs. separate filing analysis
  • ABLE account strategies for qualifying family members

Legal Document Coordination

An estate plan is only as strong as its documentation. We identify the gaps, flag inconsistencies, and coordinate with your attorney so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Incapacity trigger design (healthcare proxy, durable POA, springing POA)
  • Trustee and successor trustee selection guidance
  • Beneficiary designation reviews across all accounts
  • Estate gap analysis — identifying missing documents or inconsistent designations

Wealth Transfer

Passing wealth to the next generation is one of the most consequential financial decisions you'll make. Done right, it multiplies what you've built. Done poorly, it can trigger unnecessary taxes and family conflict.

  • Gift tax exclusion strategies
  • Stepped-up basis planning for inherited assets
  • Cross-border estate considerations (US/Greece)
  • Coordination with your estate attorney — we handle the numbers, they handle the documents

Client Story

"A family with a special needs dependent was distributing trust income without considering the impact on government benefits. After restructuring the trust's distribution strategy and electing QDT status, the family preserved full benefit eligibility while reducing the trust's annual tax burden by $8,400."

Estate planning isn't just for the wealthy — it's for anyone who wants to protect their family.
