Retirement & Wealth Planning

Stress-Tested Against
Thousands of Scenarios.

Your plan, stress-tested against thousands of scenarios — not built on a spreadsheet and a hope.

Contribution Optimization

Tax-advantaged accounts are one of the most powerful tools in your financial arsenal — but most people aren't using them to their full potential. We map out the optimal contribution waterfall for your specific situation.

  • 401(k), IRA, HSA, and Roth contribution waterfall — maximizing every tax-advantaged dollar
  • Mega backdoor Roth strategies where eligible
  • SECURE 2.0 catch-up provisions (age 50+, enhanced 60-63 window)
  • Employer match optimization and vesting schedules

Social Security Analysis

The difference between claiming Social Security at 62 versus 70 can exceed $200,000 in lifetime benefits. The right answer depends on your health, other income sources, and whether spousal or survivor benefits are in play.

  • Claiming age analysis with break-even modeling and longevity weighting
  • Spousal and survivor benefit coordination
  • Social Security taxation thresholds and provisional income management
  • NPV analysis weighted by actuarial life tables

Retirement Simulations

A single projected return is a fiction. Real planning requires modeling the full distribution of outcomes — and making sure your plan survives even the bad ones.

  • Thousands of market scenarios (bull markets, bear markets, stagflation, historical crises)
  • Multiple spending strategies — fixed, dynamic, guardrail-based, floor-and-ceiling
  • Tax-aware withdrawal sequencing (which account to draw from and when)
  • Probability of success with clear confidence intervals

Inheritance-Aware Planning

If a significant inheritance is in your future, it changes the optimal strategy for contributions, conversions, and spending. We model it explicitly — without betting on it.

  • Modeling across multiple inheritance timing and amount scenarios
  • Actuarial probability weighting for realistic planning
  • Contribution strategy that accounts for potential windfalls without relying on them

Life Trajectory Modeling

Your financial life doesn't happen in isolation. A property sale triggers capital gains, which affects your tax bracket, which affects your Roth conversion window, which affects your FI date. We model the cascades.

  • Multi-decade forward simulation incorporating major life events — property sales, career changes, children, relocation
  • Cross-domain cascades: how one decision ripples through your entire financial picture
  • Monte Carlo fan charts showing the range of possible outcomes

Client Story

"A couple in their early 50s was contributing entirely to traditional 401(k)s and planning to claim Social Security at 62. After modeling 24 combinations of contribution strategy, claiming age, and withdrawal sequence, we identified an approach that extended their portfolio longevity by 7 years — primarily through Roth conversions in early retirement and delaying Social Security to 70."

Retirement planning is too important for rules of thumb.
