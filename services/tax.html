Tax Strategy

Year-Round, Not
Filing Season.

Year-round tax planning and preparation — not just filing season.

Comprehensive Tax Planning

Most tax advice happens in April. Ours happens all year — because every financial decision you make has tax consequences, and catching them early is the difference between planning and damage control.

  • Federal income tax modeling across all brackets, filing statuses, and scenarios
  • State tax coordination (multi-state situations handled)
  • Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) awareness and avoidance strategies
  • Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) and self-employment tax optimization

Cross-Border Tax Coordination

Dual US/Greece tax situations are genuinely complex. Treaty provisions, foreign tax credits, FBAR obligations — most generalist advisors miss critical elements. We don't.

  • US/Greece dual tax situations — treaty provisions, foreign tax credits, reporting obligations
  • International asset reporting (FBAR, FATCA)
  • Coordination between US and Greek tax calendars

Proactive Tax Alpha

Tax alpha is the return generated not by picking better stocks, but by making smarter tax decisions. Every year brings specific windows for action — and missing them is money left on the table.

  • Tax-loss harvesting integrated with portfolio management
  • Roth conversion timing analysis — finding the optimal year and amount
  • QBI (Section 199A) deduction optimization for business owners
  • Entity structure impact analysis (how your business structure affects your personal taxes)

Tax Preparation

Filing is the final step of a year-round process — not a standalone event. When we prepare your return, we already know your situation inside out.

  • Every return processed through 260 automated validation checks before filing
  • Quarterly estimated payment calculations and tracking
  • Document ingestion and organization (1099s, W-2s, K-1s)
  • Multi-year tax projection for strategic decision-making
