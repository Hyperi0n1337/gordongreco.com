Business Advisory

Focus on Running
Your Business.

The right structure, the right strategy, the right numbers — so you can focus on running your business.

Entity Structure Analysis

Choosing the wrong business structure is one of the most expensive mistakes self-employed people make. We run the numbers side by side so the decision is based on actual dollars, not conventional wisdom.

  • Side-by-side comparison of sole proprietorship, LLC, and S-corporation
  • Self-employment tax vs. FICA analysis
  • QBI (Section 199A) deduction impact — including SSTB phase-out consequences
  • Payroll cost modeling for S-corp reasonable compensation

Retirement Planning for Business Owners

Business owners have access to some of the most powerful retirement contribution vehicles available — but the optimal strategy varies by entity type, income level, and goals.

  • Solo 401(k) optimization per entity type (employee + employer contribution maximization)
  • SEP-IRA alternatives and comparison
  • How entity choice affects your retirement contribution ceiling

Cash Flow Management

Knowing your business generates revenue is different from knowing where your money actually goes and when. We build the forecast that tells you both.

  • 90-day cash flow forecasting with seasonal variance modeling
  • Optimal cash buffer sizing — enough safety without leaving money idle
  • Payment routing optimization (which account pays which obligation)
  • Sweep analysis — identifying safe-to-invest surplus capital

Benefits & Threshold Management

Earning just over a threshold can cost more than earning less. ACA subsidies, IRMAA surcharges, and EITC phase-outs all create cliffs where careful income management pays off.

  • ACA subsidy (Premium Tax Credit) income threshold management
  • EITC phase-out awareness for lower-income years or startups
  • IRMAA surcharge avoidance for Medicare-age business owners

Real Asset & Property Management

If your business owns real estate, vehicles, or equipment — or if you have rental properties — the tax picture gets complex fast. We track it all and make sure you're capturing every deduction.

  • Rental property P&L tracking and tax impact
  • Depreciation schedules and recapture planning
  • Airbnb/short-term rental income analysis
  • Vehicle and equipment cost tracking

Client Story

"A consultant earning $180K through a single-member LLC was paying $25,500 in self-employment tax. After analyzing the numbers, we recommended an S-corp election with a $95K reasonable salary — reducing SE tax by $13,200 annually while maximizing Solo 401(k) contributions to shelter an additional $46,000 from income tax."

Your business structure shouldn't cost you more than it has to.
