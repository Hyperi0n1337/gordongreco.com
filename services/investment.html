Investment Management

Built Around Your
Tax Situation.

Portfolio construction and ongoing management — built around your tax situation, not a model portfolio.

Portfolio Construction

We start from your complete financial picture — tax profile, time horizon, existing holdings, and risk tolerance — and build a portfolio that optimizes for after-tax outcomes, not just gross returns.

  • Mean-variance optimization tailored to your risk tolerance and tax profile
  • Diversification across asset classes, geographies, and account types
  • Position sizing calibrated to your overall financial picture

Tax-Loss Harvesting & Tax Alpha

Tax drag is one of the most consistent destroyers of long-term wealth — and one of the most controllable. We actively monitor for opportunities to harvest losses, reset cost basis, and position holdings in the right account types.

  • Continuous monitoring for harvesting opportunities with automated wash-sale detection
  • Gain harvesting in low-bracket years to reset cost basis
  • Qualified dividend positioning in tax-advantaged accounts
  • Asset location optimization — the right holdings in the right account types (taxable, tax-deferred, tax-free)

Risk Management

We don't just model expected returns — we stress-test your portfolio against the scenarios that actually destroy wealth. Understanding how your holdings behave in a crisis is as important as knowing how they behave in a bull market.

  • Stress testing against historical crises (2008 GFC, COVID 2020) and hypothetical scenarios (rate shocks, equity crashes)
  • Drawdown analysis and recovery projections
  • Margin and leverage monitoring with defined guardrails

Reporting & Transparency

You should always know what's in your portfolio, why it's there, and how it's performing. Our reports are written for people, not auditors.

  • Quarterly performance reports with benchmark comparison and full attribution
  • Holdings breakdown by account, asset class, and tax lot
  • Clear explanation of every trade and its rationale

Client Story

"A client with $1.2M spread across four accounts — a 401(k), two IRAs, and a taxable brokerage — was unknowingly holding high-yield bond funds in their taxable account while growth stocks sat in their traditional IRA. By restructuring asset location alone, we reduced their annual tax drag by approximately $14,000 — without changing their overall allocation or risk profile."
